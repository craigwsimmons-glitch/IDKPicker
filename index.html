Common questions

What is IDKPicker?
A free AI-powered restaurant picker. Give it your location and vibe — it picks one perfect place, no endless scrolling required.
How does it work?
It uses your GPS to find nearby restaurants via Google Places, then Claude AI picks the best match for your mood, cuisine, and budget.
Is it free?
Yes — completely free, no account or sign-up needed.
Where does it work?
Anywhere Google Places has data — most cities worldwide.
What if I don't like the pick?
Tap Pick Again — it automatically excludes the previous pick so you always get a fresh suggestion.
